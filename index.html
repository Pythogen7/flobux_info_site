We believe in making financial management accessible to everyone. Our app is 100% free – no subscriptions, no premium tiers, no ads. The only cost is for linking external bank accounts, which is a fee we can't avoid from our banking partners.

Our goal is not to turn a profit. We aim to cover necessary costs—including server expenses, bank integration fees, and fair salaries for those working to maintain and improve the app. Pricing will always reflect providing the best service to all, not making a profit.
